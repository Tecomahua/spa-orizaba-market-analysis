# Project overview

## Research question

Could publicly available economic, demographic, and establishment data help frame an initial market discussion for a wellness SPA in Orizaba, Veracruz?

## Sources

- ENIGH 2024 for state and national economic context.
- DENUE for registered SPA-related establishments.
- Census 2020 / ITER for municipal demographic context.

The sources describe different populations and concepts. Their values should not be combined into a direct local-demand estimate.

## Technical contribution

My work centered on finding the data, cleaning and transforming it, joining the sources needed for the analysis, calculating indicators, and preparing technical outputs for the report. The team’s storytelling and communication were handled by other members.

## Report context

The May 2026 team report states 123,182 residents for Orizaba, 26 direct SPA establishments in the municipality, and 28 in nearby municipalities. Those values are preserved as reported. This showcase does not claim to reproduce the underlying analysis independently.

## Boundaries

The study is an exploratory academic assessment. It does not estimate revenue, consumer demand, ROI, or business success. The available source repository does not reproduce the report’s suggested-zone score, so the score is not presented here as a verified result.
